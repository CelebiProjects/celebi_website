We have built our workflow in guide 1 and 2. However, we can only process data and get some output files, but we can't see directly what we get, nor 
do we know where these files are stored on the server. To solve this problem, we are proud to provide an extension for VSCode. Using this extension,
we can see details of our tasks clearly, preview all output files and download them if necessary.

## 1. Download Celebi Extension for VSCode
Search for "Celebi" in Extensions and download it.

<img width="824" height="314" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e2fc41d0-8431-4c79-894f-d98539e29057" />



## 2. Overview of the Extension

<img width="928" height="760" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87092320-57a8-47aa-a54f-c95f0400837f" />

After Downloading the extension, a Celebi sign will appear on the left, as is shown in the figure. Click on it and the celebi panel will appear.
You can see all your folders and the tasks here. If you click on a task, it will show only the programmes, so it is very easy to have a good grasp of the 
workflow.

If you have started yuki and celebi, click on the fresh buttton on top right corner and "DITE" will show "connected".

## 3. Details of a certain Task

If you click on a task, its details will show on the right.

<img width="1910" height="1334" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4271ab4d-3d75-416b-96c2-d1b23b50c2fa" />

You may edit the "README.MD" to explain what this task is for. It is highly suggested to write README for every task.

JOB STATUS shows the status of the task, the requested runner. 

PREDECESSORS show the upstream tasks for this task. Click on the sign and you can 
directly move to the corrsponding task. SUCCESSORS likewise show the downstream tasks.

You can edit the CELEBI.YAML to change the settings and commands of the task.

RESULTS is the most important part. Here you can preview all kinds of files. Take root files as an example. Click on the root file, it shows:

<img width="1518" height="404" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/94bb3e92-7be2-43f1-9027-6461d243a442" />

Click on the "DecayTree" and choose a parameter, its distribution will appear on the right after a few seconds.

<img width="1850" height="1556" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a27ff88-1fe4-462d-b39d-de6af98b59fa" />

If the output is pdf, it will directly show there. To see the full image, just click on it.
